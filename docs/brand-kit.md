# Brand & printables

Original starter FF monogram: two F forms on a shared foundation. Assets live in public/brand; logo letterforms are outlined, SVG backgrounds transparent. Mark-only artwork is used in the site header. Charcoal #242622, orange #F16A2B, paper #F3F1E9. Typography outlines derive from DejaVu Sans Condensed Bold. No font files are distributed.

public/downloads contains three US Letter PDF kits (church promotion, notebook, brand/apparel guidance), first-page previews, and the complete ZIP. These are checked-in static files; the build needs no Python or PDF dependencies.

The Downloads page exposes originals and a browser-only church-name customizer. Church text is set with textContent, never interpreted as markup, and is not saved or sent. Exported church-name text remains live: the printer should outline it before reproduction. Black/white variants support light/dark substrates. SVG sizes are scalable; placement, print size, and production proofing belong to the garment supplier. No store, payments, supplier connection, or trademark clearance is included.

Churches and participants may reproduce these materials for Foundation Framework activities and local apparel. Curriculum remains sample content. A future ordering integration should use approved product placements and deliberate supplier selection rather than treat the downloadable art as an existing store.
